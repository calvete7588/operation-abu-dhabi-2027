**Date:** August 18, 2026

## Objective
Master the OSI model, TCP/IP protocols, firewall filtering behaviors (stateful vs. stateless), network segmentation, proxy servers, and wireless security standards (WPA/Wi-Fi).

## Core Concepts Explained
* **Addressing & Resolution Protocols:**
  * *ARP:* Resolves IP addresses to physical MAC addresses within a local network segment.
  * *DNS:* Translates human-readable domain names into IP addresses.
* **Network Defense & Perimeter Controls:**
  * *Firewalls (Stateful vs. Stateless):* Stateful tracks active connection contexts; Stateless applies static rule sets per packet.
  * *Proxies (Forward vs. Reverse):* Forward regulates outbound client requests; Reverse shields internal servers from inbound external traffic.
  * *Network Segmentation & Subnetting:* Isolating controlled/uncontrolled zones and subnets to prevent lateral movement.
* **Secure Protocols & Encryption:**
  * *Secure Communications:* Standardized usage of SSH (Port 22), SFTP, HTTPS (Port 443), and VPN encapsulation.
  * *Wireless Security:* IEEE 802.11 standards and WPA protocols for securing WLAN traffic.

## Technical Vocabulary
* **Stateful Inspection** (/stéit-ful in-spék-shon/): Inspección con seguimiento de estado de conexión en firewalls.
* **Address Resolution Protocol (ARP)** (/ád-res re-so-lú-shon pró-to-kol/): Protocolo de resolución de direcciones IP a MAC.
* **Reverse Proxy** (/ri-vérs prók-si/): Servidor proxy inverso para la protección de servidores internos.
