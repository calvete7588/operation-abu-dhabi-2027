## Objective
Analyze network perimeter defenses, differentiate active vs. passive intrusion systems (IDS/IPS), master DMZ segmentation and VPN protocols, and complete Course 3 of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate.

## Core Concepts Explained
* **Perimeter Architecture & Isolation:**
  * *Demilitarized Zone (DMZ):* Isolated subnetwork hosting public services to prevent direct entry to the internal corporate LAN.
* **Monitoring & Intrusion Handling:**
  * *IDS (Intrusion Detection System):* Passive sensor for anomaly detection and alert generation.
  * *IPS (Intrusion Prevention System):* Active control placed inline to inspect and block malicious traffic in real time.
* **Encrypted Remote Access:**
  * *VPN (Virtual Private Network):* Secure tunnel establishing confidentiality via IPsec/TLS encryption over untrusted networks.
* **Course 3 Summary:** Fully mastered network fundamentals (LAN/WAN, TCP/IP, OSI), network protocols (DNS, ARP, SSH), hardening practices, and attack vectors (DoS/DDoS, Spoofing, Sniffing).

## Technical Vocabulary
* **Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)** (/in-trú-shon pri-vén-shon sís-tem/): Sistema activo de prevención de intrusos.
* **Demilitarized Zone (DMZ)** (/di-mí-li-ta-ráizd zóun/): Subred aislada para servidores públicos.
* **IPsec** (/ai-pi-sek/): Protocolo de seguridad e inspección de datos cifrados en red.
